## Scribe 0.16.4

- **Google Meet is now recognized.** Scribe notices when you join a Google Meet call in your browser the same way it does for Zoom: it can offer to record when the call starts, ask whether to stop when it ends, and post the written notice into the Meet chat. Works with Chrome and other Chromium browsers (Edge, Brave, Arc); it needs the same Accessibility permission the Zoom chat notice uses.
- **The chat notice is far more dependable.** Scribe now recognizes the meeting from Zoom's own window, so it works however you get into the meeting: through Join & Record, by clicking a link, from the Zoom app, through a waiting room, or while you're already sharing your screen. It waits until you have actually joined (the preview with the Join button no longer counts), finds the chat box whether the panel is docked or popped out, confirms the message really went through before it stops trying, and keeps trying for up to ten minutes after you start recording. It never posts the same notice twice.
- **Use GitHub Copilot with your meetings.** Install Scribe Meetings for GitHub Copilot from Settings and Copilot can search your transcripts, answer questions about your meetings, and write minutes, in VS Code (type /scribe-meetings.write_minutes in chat) and in the Copilot command-line tool. Your transcripts stay on your Mac. You can also have the Copilot command-line tool write minutes automatically after each recording: choose it under Settings, Meeting Minutes.
- **Add a Scribe agent to Microsoft 365 Copilot.** If your company uses Microsoft 365 Copilot, Scribe can create a Scribe Meetings agent for Teams, Outlook, and the Copilot app. Keep your transcripts folder in OneDrive and the agent can write minutes, catch you up, build a weekly digest, and answer questions about your meetings. Upload the package Scribe creates in Teams, or ask your IT admin to.
- **Pick your AI when you set up Scribe.** The welcome guide now lets you connect Claude, Codex, GitHub Copilot, or Microsoft 365 Copilot, with Claude selected by default.
- **Move the recording badge wherever you like.** The small "Scribe" pill that shows while recording now starts in the bottom-left corner and can be dragged anywhere on the screen. Scribe remembers where you put it, on every display and for every future recording.
- **The "record this meeting?" card comes back for your next meeting.** Recording, dismissing, or choosing a title on the card now only covers the meeting it was for. As soon as that meeting ends, the next one you join gets its own offer, even a few minutes later.
