## Scribe 0.16.8

- **Scribe Remote is on the App Store.** Settings now links straight to the free iPhone app, which starts, pauses and stops recordings, takes snapshots, marks moments, and follows the live transcript from your phone.
- **Snapshots are labeled for what they do.** A snapshot captures the Zoom meeting window, or your whole screen in any other meeting app, so its status now simply reads "Snapshot" instead of "Zoom snapshot".
