## Scribe 0.16.5

- **A new welcome guide.** Setting up Scribe is now a short, step-by-step guide: see where Scribe lives in your menu bar, allow the two permissions it needs, choose where your transcripts are saved, and connect your AI of choice (Claude, Codex, GitHub Copilot, or Microsoft 365 Copilot). You can jump back to any step from the sidebar, skip the optional ones, and if you close the guide partway through it picks up where you left off. Reopen it any time from Settings, Show Welcome Guide.
- **The menu bar icon shows what Scribe is doing.** While recording, the icon now turns red and gently pulses, and it turns orange while paused, so you can tell at a glance whether you're being recorded. Previously it stayed white in every state.
- **A cleaner Scribe menu.** The top of the menu now shows the Scribe name and icon. A note appears beneath it only when something needs your attention, such as the microphone being unavailable.
