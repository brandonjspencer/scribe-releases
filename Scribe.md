## Scribe 0.17.1

- **Free meetings now run out after 20.** Scribe has always counted your free meetings; from this version, once the 20 are used, starting a new recording asks for the one-time $9.99 license. Everything you've already recorded (transcripts, minutes, search) stays yours and keeps working, and a recording that's in progress is never interrupted.
- **Already past 20? You get 3 more.** If you've recorded more than 20 meetings already, you have 3 more free meetings from this update before Scribe asks for a license.
- **A heads-up before it happens.** When two free meetings are left, a small card in the Scribe panel tells you how many free meetings are left and where to unlock. You can dismiss it.
- **Already have a license?** Nothing changes for you. If you were using Scribe before licenses existed, you're still unlimited.
