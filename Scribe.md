## Scribe 0.17.0

- **See how many free meetings you have left.** The number now sits next to the Scribe name at the top of the menu bar window, with an Unlock link beside it that takes you straight to your license.
- **Scribe can open when you log in.** New installs turn it on at the end of setup, so meeting reminders and Join & Record keep working after a restart. For everyone else it's a new switch in Settings, and it stays off until you turn it on.
- **Minutes are on by default for new installs.** When you finish a recording, Scribe now writes the minutes for you, as long as Claude Code or GitHub Copilot CLI is installed. Nothing changes for current users, and you can switch it off in Settings.
- **Tell us how Scribe is working.** After your fifth meeting, a small card in the menu bar window offers a short feedback form. It appears once and goes away whichever button you press.
