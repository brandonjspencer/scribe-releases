Getting started

  1. Download the DMG and drag Scribe into Applications.
  2. Launch it — Scribe lives in your menu bar (look for the waveform icon near the clock).
  3. Follow the welcome guide to grant Screen & System Audio Recording and Microphone permissions.
  4. Click Start Recording in a meeting. Transcripts land in your chosen folder as plain text.

Pricing

Frequently asked

What counts as one of the 20 free meetings?
Any completed recording of 2 minutes or longer. Shorter test recordings are free and don't count, and a recording already in progress is never interrupted by the count.
Do I keep what I already recorded if I don't buy a license?
Yes. Every transcript, your minutes, the Minutes Reader, Ask your meetings, search, and export all keep working with no license. Only starting a new recording is gated.
How many Macs can I use one license on?
Up to 3 at a time. Deactivate one from Settings → License to free a slot for another.
Do I pay again for updates?
No. Every point release of the major version you bought (for example, all of Scribe 1.x) is a free update forever, delivered automatically.
What if I was already using Scribe before it had a license?
You're grandfathered in automatically — recording stays unlimited on Macs where Scribe already had transcripts before the license shipped. Nothing to do, no code needed.
Scribe updates itself automatically via a signed update feed.
Everything is processed on-device. Recording conversations may require participant consent where you live — announce it.
