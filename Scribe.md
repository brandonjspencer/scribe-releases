## Scribe 0.16.9

- **Scribe now keeps itself up to date.** Updates download in the background and install on their own while Scribe is idle, never during a recording or a meeting, and Scribe reopens by itself a moment later. You'll get fixes without having to notice an update window. To turn this off, go to Settings, Updates, and switch off "Install updates automatically".
- **Update windows can't hide anymore.** When an update does need your OK, its window now comes to the front instead of opening behind other apps.
- **Includes the chat-notice fix from 0.16.7.** If your meetings still showed Scribe's chat notice twice, this update stops that. It's posted once per meeting.
